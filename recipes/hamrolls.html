Ham rolls

This is my favorite lasagna recipe.

Ham rolls are a quick and easy cold appetizer made with slices of ham rolled around a creamy filling, usually cream cheese and sliced cheese. They're held together with toothpicks and served chilled, making them perfect for parties, snacks, or picnics.

ingredients

  • Sliced ham
  • Cream cheese
  • Sliced cheese
  • Toothpicks

Directions

  1. Lay the ham slices flat.
  2. Spread a thin layer of cream cheese on each slice.
  3. Place a slice of cheese on top.
  4. Roll them up tightly.
  5. Secure each roll with a toothpick.
  6. Chill in the fridge for 15 minutes and serve.
