Brownies

Brownies are a rich, dense chocolate dessert baked in a square pan and cut into small squares. They have a shiny, crackly top and a soft, fudgy center. They're made with butter, sugar, eggs, flour, and chocolate or cocoa, and are often served with ice cream or nuts.

ingredients

  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Flour
  • Cocoa powder
  • Chocolate
  • Salt

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Melt the butter and chocolate together.
  3. Mix in the sugar, then add the eggs one at a time.
  4. Add the flour, cocoa powder, and a pinch of salt. Stir well.
  5. Pour the mix into a greased baking pan.
  6. Bake for 20-25 minutes.
  7. Let them cool, then cut into squares.
