Lasagna

This is my favorite lasagna recipe.

Lasagna is a traditional baked dish made with alternating layers of pasta sheets, meat sauce or ragù, béchamel sauce, and cheese for browning on top.

ingredients

  • lasagna sheets
  • Ground meat
  • Onion
  • Crushed tomatoes
  • Bechamel Sauce
  • Grated Cheese
  • Salt And oil

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Fry the onion in oil, then add the meat and cook until brown.
  3. Add the crushed tomatoes and salt. Cook for 15 minutes.
  4. In a baking dish, add a layer of sauce, then lasagna sheets, meat, and béchamel.
  5. Repeat the layers.
  6. Finish with béchamel and grated cheese on top.
  7. Bake for 30-40 minutes until golden.
